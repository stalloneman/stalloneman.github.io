## Overview

The bill of materials for the RadioLink hub is [*RadioLink-Hub-BOM-detailed-v3.xlsx*](RadioLink-Hub-BOM-detailed-v3.xlsx). It uses the course columns and is sorted by supplier.

Parts from the PRLTA 109 drawers have a prototype cost of $0 and are not ordered. Purchased parts come from BuyDisplay and DigiKey. Local datasheets are [ER-OLED0.96-1_Series_Datasheet.pdf](datasheets/ER-OLED0.96-1_Series_Datasheet.pdf), [2N7000.pdf](datasheets/2N7000.pdf), and [MCP1700.pdf](datasheets/MCP1700.pdf).
